Universal Downloader — Data Subject Rights

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This page explains how users can exercise privacy rights relating to information processed by Universal Downloader.

Rights you may have

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to personal data associated with you, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of data, restriction or objection to certain processing, or information about how data is used.

Information that may be involved

Universal Downloader may process limited technical information such as an app-generated installation identifier, referral information, trial or Premium status, app version, and diagnostics. The app does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call history, IMEI, or device serial number.

How to submit a request

Open Universal Downloader and use Contact Us or Report a Problem. State that the message is a privacy or data-rights request and describe what you want us to access, correct, or delete. Include only the minimum information needed to identify the relevant app installation or support report.

Local data

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing Universal Downloader's app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This does not automatically delete records retained independently by an app store or payment provider.

App-store and purchase records

Subscription and transaction records handled by an app store are controlled under that store's own privacy and retention policies. Requests concerning those records may need to be submitted directly to the relevant store provider.

Verification and response

We may ask for reasonable information to verify a request and prevent unauthorized access or deletion. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy law.
