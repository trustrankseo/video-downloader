Universal Downloader Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Universal Downloader is developed by Zubair Abbas. This policy explains what information the app may process and how it is used.

Information processed

The app may process an app-generated installation identifier, referral source or referral code, trial and premium entitlement state, app version, basic device/app diagnostics, and download status needed to operate the app. The app does not require a user account and does not request a user's name, precise location, contacts, SMS, call history, IMEI, or device serial number.

Media links and downloads

Links entered by the user are processed only to perform the requested download or discovery operation. Downloaded files are saved locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right or authorization to download and save the content they request.

Purchases

If Premium membership is offered, purchase and subscription processing is handled by the applicable app store or payment provider. Universal Downloader does not receive or store full payment-card details.

Diagnostics and support

If a user chooses to send a problem report, the app may include app version, device/app diagnostics, and the issue description supplied by the user. A report is not sent unless the user chooses to submit it.

Sharing

Personal data is not sold. Information may be processed by app-store, billing, or technical service providers only as needed to provide app functionality and subject to their own privacy terms.

Retention

Local app settings and identifiers remain on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed, except where an app-store service keeps transaction records under its own policies.

Your rights

Users may request access, correction, deletion, or information about data associated with their use of Universal Downloader where applicable. See the Data Subject Rights page.

Children

Universal Downloader is not designed as a kids-only app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the in-app Contact Us or Report a Problem option.
