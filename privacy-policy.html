Universal Downloader Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Universal Downloader is provided by Zubair Abbas. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information handled by the app

The app may process limited technical information needed to operate its features, such as an app-generated installation identifier, referral attribution, app version, trial usage, premium membership status, and diagnostic information submitted by you when reporting a problem.

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect your precise location, contacts, SMS messages, call history, or raw device identifiers such as IMEI or serial number.

Downloaded content

URLs you provide are processed only to perform the requested download or collection discovery. Downloaded files are saved locally on your device. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right or authorization to download and use any content.

Payments

If premium membership is offered, purchase processing is handled by the relevant app store payment service. The app may receive purchase status or entitlement information but does not receive your full payment card details.

Referral and device eligibility

The app may use an app-generated installation identifier and referral information to determine trial and referral eligibility and to reduce abuse such as repeated self-referrals or repeated local trial claims.

Problem reports

If you choose to use the Report a Problem feature, your report may include the issue details you provide together with basic app and device diagnostics needed to investigate the issue.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Limited information may be processed by app-store, billing, hosting, or technical service providers only where needed to operate the app and its services.

Retention

Local app data remains on your device until removed by the app, by you, or through app uninstallation. Any support information you voluntarily send may be retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and maintain service records.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of the app. See the Data Subject Rights page for details.

Children

Universal Downloader is not designed as a kids-only app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

Contact

You can contact the developer through the in-app Contact Us or Report a Problem feature.
