## English

### 1. Information the app may process

Universal Downloader is designed to minimize data collection. Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

- An app-generated installation identifier used for local trial, referral, eligibility, and anti-abuse controls.
- Referral information such as referral code, referral source, referral status, and reward state.
- App and device technical information such as app version, Android version, device model, and basic diagnostics when needed for troubleshooting.
- Premium entitlement or purchase status when paid membership features are used. Full payment-card details are not collected by Universal Downloader; payments are handled by the applicable app store or payment provider.
- URLs that you paste into the app for downloading supported media. These URLs are processed only to perform the requested download or discovery action.
- Optional problem-report information that you choose to send, including your written description and technical diagnostics included in the report.

The app does not request your name, precise location, contacts, SMS, call history, IMEI, or device serial number.

### 2. How information is used

Information is used only to:

- Provide Single, Bulk, Channel, Playlist, Profile, MP4, and MP3 download functions where supported.
- Manage free trials, referral eligibility, rewards, and Premium access.
- Detect duplicate or abusive trial/referral use.
- Maintain app settings such as appearance preferences.
- Diagnose crashes, failed downloads, and user-reported problems.
- Meet store, security, legal, and compliance requirements.

### 3. Local storage and downloaded files

Downloaded files are saved on the user's device, normally under the UniversalDownloader folder in the device Downloads directory. Local settings, trial state, referral state, and other app preferences may also be stored on the device.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app information, subject to Android and app-store behavior.

### 4. Payments and app stores

If Premium membership or another paid feature is offered, payment processing is handled by the relevant app store or payment provider, including **HUAWEI AppGallery** where applicable. Universal Downloader does not receive or store full payment-card information. The app may receive purchase status, product information, transaction references, or entitlement information needed to activate and restore Premium features.

The app store's own privacy policy also applies to information processed by that store.

### 5. Third-party websites and media sources

Universal Downloader processes links supplied by the user and may connect to third-party websites or media platforms only as needed to perform the requested function. Those third-party services operate independently and have their own terms and privacy practices.

Users are responsible for downloading only content they own, are authorized to download, or are otherwise permitted to save under applicable law and the terms of the relevant service.

### 6. Sharing of personal data

Universal Downloader does **not sell personal data**. Information may be processed or shared only when necessary with service providers used to operate app functionality, process purchases, respond to support requests, comply with law, or protect users and the app from abuse.

### 7. Data retention

Local app data is generally retained on the device until it is cleared, reset, or removed. Support or diagnostic information voluntarily sent by the user may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the issue, maintain records, or comply with legal obligations.

Purchase and entitlement records may be retained by the relevant app store according to that store's policies and legal requirements.

### 8. Children's privacy

Universal Downloader is not designed as a children's-only app. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

### 9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss of information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

### 10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other control over personal information associated with the app.

### 11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, legal requirements, store requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

## 简体中文（中国大陆适用）

### 1. 本应用可能处理的信息

Universal Downloader 以尽量少收集数据为原则。根据您使用的功能，本应用可能处理：

- 由应用生成的安装标识符，用于本地试用、推荐、资格判断和防滥用控制；
- 推荐信息，例如推荐码、推荐来源、推荐状态及奖励状态；
- 应用和设备的基本技术信息，例如应用版本、Android 版本、设备型号，以及在故障排查时所需的基础诊断信息；
- 使用付费功能时的 Premium 权益或购买状态。Universal Downloader 不收集完整的银行卡信息；支付由相应的应用商店或支付服务提供方处理；
- 您粘贴到应用中的媒体链接。这些链接仅用于执行您主动请求的下载或内容发现操作；
- 您主动选择发送的问题报告，包括您填写的问题描述以及报告中包含的基础技术诊断信息。

本应用不会要求提供您的姓名、精确位置、联系人、短信、通话记录、IMEI 或设备序列号。

### 2. 信息的使用目的

相关信息仅用于：

- 在支持的情况下提供单个下载、批量下载、频道、播放列表、主页、MP4 和 MP3 功能；
- 管理免费试用、推荐资格、奖励和 Premium 权益；
- 识别重复或滥用试用/推荐功能的行为；
- 保存应用设置，例如界面主题偏好；
- 排查崩溃、下载失败及用户主动报告的问题；
- 满足应用商店、安全、法律及合规要求。

### 3. 本地存储和下载文件

下载的文件保存在用户设备本地，通常位于设备“Downloads/UniversalDownloader”目录。应用设置、试用状态、推荐状态及其他应用偏好也可能保存在设备本地。

清除应用数据或卸载应用可能会删除本地保存的信息，具体取决于 Android 系统及应用商店的处理方式。

### 4. 支付与应用商店

如提供 Premium 会员或其他付费功能，付款和订阅由相应的应用商店或支付服务提供方处理；在适用情况下包括 **HUAWEI AppGallery**。Universal Downloader 不接收或保存完整的银行卡信息。本应用可能接收开通或恢复 Premium 所必需的购买状态、商品信息、交易参考信息或权益状态。

应用商店自行处理的信息同时受该应用商店自身隐私政策约束。

### 5. 第三方网站和媒体来源

Universal Downloader 会处理用户主动提供的链接，并仅在执行用户请求的功能所必需的情况下连接相应的第三方网站或媒体服务。第三方服务独立运营，并适用其各自的服务条款及隐私规则。

用户应仅下载自己拥有、已获得授权，或根据适用法律及相关服务条款允许保存的内容。

### 6. 个人信息的共享

Universal Downloader **不会出售个人信息**。仅在提供应用功能、处理购买、响应支持请求、遵守法律要求或防止滥用所必需的情况下，相关信息才可能由相应服务提供方处理或共享。

### 7. 保存期限

应用本地数据通常保存在设备上，直至用户清除、重置或卸载应用。用户主动发送的支持或诊断信息仅在调查问题、保留必要记录或履行法律义务所合理需要的期限内保存。

购买和权益记录可能由相应应用商店根据其政策及法律要求保存。

### 8. 未成年人隐私

Universal Downloader 并非专门面向儿童设计的应用。开发者不会通过本应用主动要求儿童提供个人信息。

### 9. 安全措施

我们采取合理的技术和管理措施，以降低信息遭到未经授权访问、修改、披露或丢失的风险。但任何电子存储或传输方式均无法保证绝对安全。
