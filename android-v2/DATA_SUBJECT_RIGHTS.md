# Data Subject Rights — Universal Downloader

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

This page explains how users of **Universal Downloader**, developed by **Zubair Abbas**, can exercise privacy and data-protection rights that may apply in their country or region.

## Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

- Ask whether personal information relating to you is being processed.
- Request access to personal information associated with your use of the app.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of information where legally permitted.
- Request restriction of certain processing.
- Object to certain processing.
- Request information about the source, purpose, or categories of data processed.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Request portability where required by applicable law.
- Lodge a complaint with an applicable data-protection authority.

## Information that may be relevant to a request

Universal Downloader is designed to minimize data collection. Depending on the features used, relevant information may include an app-generated installation identifier, referral status, trial or reward state, app/device diagnostics, Premium entitlement status, and information voluntarily supplied in a support or problem report.

Downloaded media files and most app settings are stored locally on the user's device. Universal Downloader does not collect full payment-card details.

## How to make a request

To submit a privacy-rights request:

1. Use the developer contact information shown on the **Universal Downloader** listing in **HUAWEI AppGallery**.
2. State that the message is a **Data Subject Rights Request**.
3. Include the app name: **Universal Downloader**.
4. Describe the right you want to exercise, such as access, correction, or deletion.
5. Provide only the minimum information needed to identify the relevant app record. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or unrelated sensitive information.

If verification is reasonably necessary to protect users from unauthorized requests, the developer may ask for limited additional information before acting on the request.

## Local data on your device

Some information is stored only on your device. You can generally remove local app data through Android settings by clearing Universal Downloader's app data or by uninstalling the app. Downloaded files stored in the device Downloads/UniversalDownloader folder may need to be deleted separately by the user.

## Purchase information

If Premium membership is purchased through HUAWEI AppGallery or another app store, some purchase and transaction information is controlled by that store. Requests concerning payment-account or transaction data held by the store may need to be directed to the relevant store provider.

## Response and retention

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law. Information provided for a privacy request may be retained only as reasonably necessary to process the request, maintain required records, prevent fraud or abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
