# Privacy Policy — Universal Downloader

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

Universal Downloader is developed and published by **Zubair Abbas**. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use Universal Downloader.

## 1. Information the app may process

Universal Downloader is designed to minimize data collection. Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

- An app-generated installation identifier used for local trial, referral, eligibility, and anti-abuse controls.
- Referral information such as a referral code, referral source, referral status, and reward state.
- App and device technical information such as app version, Android version, device model, and basic diagnostic information when needed for troubleshooting.
- Premium entitlement or purchase status when paid membership features are used. Payment card details are not collected by Universal Downloader; payments are handled by the applicable app store or payment provider.
- URLs that you paste into the app for downloading supported media. These URLs are processed to perform the requested download.
- Optional problem-report information that you choose to send, including your written description and technical diagnostics included in the report.

## 2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide download functions, manage free trials/referrals/Premium access, prevent abusive trial or referral use, save app settings, diagnose problems, and meet store/security/legal requirements.

## 3. Local storage and downloaded files

Downloaded files are saved on the user's device, normally under the UniversalDownloader folder in the device Downloads directory. Local settings, trial state, referral state, and other app preferences may also be stored on the device.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app information, subject to Android and app-store behavior.

## 4. Payments and app stores

If Premium membership or another paid feature is offered, payment processing is handled by the relevant app store or payment provider, such as HUAWEI AppGallery. Universal Downloader does not receive or store full payment-card information. The app may receive purchase status, product information, transaction references, or entitlement information needed to activate and restore Premium features.

## 5. Third-party websites and media sources

Universal Downloader processes links supplied by the user and may connect to third-party websites or media platforms only as needed to perform the requested function. Those third-party services operate independently and have their own terms and privacy practices.

Users are responsible for downloading only content they own, are authorized to download, or are otherwise permitted to save under applicable law and the terms of the relevant service.

## 6. Sharing of personal data

Universal Downloader does **not sell personal data**. Information may be shared only when necessary with service providers used to operate app functionality, process purchases, respond to support requests, comply with law, or protect users and the app from abuse.

## 7. Data retention

Local app data is generally retained on the device until it is cleared, reset, or removed. Support or diagnostic information voluntarily sent by the user may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the issue, maintain records, or comply with legal obligations.

## 8. Children's privacy

Universal Downloader is not intended only for children and is not designed as a children's app. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

## 9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss of information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
