Privacy Policy — Universal Downloader

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Universal Downloader is developed and published by Zubair Abbas.

Independent-app notice: Universal Downloader is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with any third-party social-media or media platform. Download only content you own or are authorized to save.

1. Information the app may process

The app may process an app-generated installation ID; referral source, code and status; Android version, app version, device model and basic diagnostics; URLs entered for download operations; ad-related device/app signals and an advertising identifier where available; and information you voluntarily include in a support report. The app does not request precise location, contacts, SMS, call history, IMEI or device serial number.

2. Purposes

Information is used to provide supported download functions, manage referrals and preferences, diagnose failures, respond to support requests, and support advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and ad-frequency control.

3. Advertising and AppLovin MAX

After you accept this Privacy Policy, the app may initialize AppLovin MAX. A banner may be displayed on the Downloader screen and an occasional interstitial may be displayed after a successful completed download operation. AppLovin and its advertising partners may process device/app signals and an advertising identifier where available for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and frequency control. Version 1.5.0 requests non-personalized advertising by default. AppLovin and advertising partners operate under their own privacy terms.

4. URLs, downloads and third-party services

URLs you enter are processed only to perform the requested download or discovery operation and may be sent to the relevant third-party media service. Downloaded files are stored locally on your device. Third-party websites and media services operate independently under their own terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing and sale

Universal Downloader does not sell personal data. Information may be processed only as necessary by media-source, advertising or technical service providers to perform requested functions, deliver and measure ads, provide support, comply with law or prevent abuse.

6. Retention

Local settings, identifiers and referral state remain on your device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Downloaded files remain until you delete them. Ad providers may retain advertising or measurement records under their own policies. Voluntary support information is retained only as reasonably necessary to investigate a request or meet legal obligations.

7. Problem reports and support

A report is sent only when you choose to open and send it through your email app. It may contain your description, app version, Android version, device model, installation ID, referral source and basic diagnostics. The app does not silently upload problem reports.

8. Children's privacy

Universal Downloader is not designed as a children's app. The developer does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your rights and advertising controls

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent for personal information associated with the app. You can clear local app data in Android settings, delete downloaded files separately, and use device privacy or advertising controls where available.

11. Policy changes

This policy may change when app functions, legal requirements, store requirements or data practices change. The effective date will be updated for material changes.
