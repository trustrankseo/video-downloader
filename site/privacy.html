English

Universal Downloader is developed by Zubair Abbas. This policy explains what information the app may process and how it is used.

1. Information processed

The app may process an app-generated installation identifier, referral source or referral code, trial and Premium entitlement state, app version, Android version, device model, basic app/device diagnostics, and URLs entered by the user to perform requested downloads or discovery actions. If the user chooses to send a problem report, the report may contain the user's description and basic diagnostics.

The app does not request a user's name, precise location, contacts, SMS, call history, IMEI, or device serial number.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide supported Single, Bulk, Channel/Playlist/Profile, MP4 and MP3 features; manage trials, referrals and Premium access; save app preferences; prevent duplicate trial/referral abuse; troubleshoot problems voluntarily reported by the user; and meet security, legal and store requirements.

3. Media links and downloaded files

Links entered by the user are processed only to perform the requested download or discovery operation. Downloaded files are saved locally on the user's device, normally in the Downloads/UniversalDownloader folder. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right or authorization to download and save the content they request.

4. Purchases

If Premium membership is offered, purchase and subscription processing is handled by the applicable app store or payment provider, including HUAWEI AppGallery where applicable. Universal Downloader does not receive or store full payment-card details. The app may receive purchase or entitlement status required to activate or restore Premium.

5. Third-party services

Universal Downloader may connect to third-party websites or media services only as needed to perform a user-requested function. Those services operate independently under their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Sharing and sale of personal data

Universal Downloader does not sell personal data. Information may be processed or shared only where necessary to provide app functionality, process purchases, respond to support requests, comply with law, or protect the app and users from abuse.

7. Retention

Local app settings and identifiers generally remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is removed. Support or diagnostic information voluntarily sent by the user is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the issue or meet legal obligations. App-store transaction records are governed by the relevant store's policies.

8. Children

Universal Downloader is not designed as a children's-only app, and the developer does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other control over personal information associated with the app.

简体中文（中国大陆适用）

Universal Downloader 由 Zubair Abbas 开发。本隐私政策说明您使用 Universal Downloader 时，本应用可能处理哪些信息以及如何使用这些信息。

1. 本应用可能处理的信息

根据您使用的功能，本应用可能处理：由应用生成的安装标识符、推荐来源或推荐码、试用及 Premium 权益状态、应用版本、Android 版本、设备型号、基础应用/设备诊断信息，以及您为执行下载或内容发现操作而输入的链接。如果您主动选择发送问题报告，报告可能包含您填写的问题描述和基础诊断信息。

本应用不会要求提供您的姓名、精确位置、联系人、短信、通话记录、IMEI 或设备序列号。

2. 信息的使用目的

相关信息仅用于：在支持的情况下提供单个下载、批量下载、频道/播放列表/主页、MP4 和 MP3 功能；管理试用、推荐和 Premium 权益；保存应用偏好；防止重复或滥用试用/推荐功能；处理您主动报告的问题；以及满足安全、法律和应用商店要求。

3. 媒体链接及下载文件

您输入的链接仅用于执行您主动请求的下载或内容发现操作。下载文件保存在您的设备本地，通常位于 Downloads/UniversalDownloader 目录。请仅下载您拥有、已获得授权，或根据适用法律及相关服务条款允许保存的内容。

4. 购买与订阅

如提供 Premium 会员，购买和订阅由相应的应用商店或支付服务提供方处理；在适用情况下包括 HUAWEI AppGallery。Universal Downloader 不接收或保存完整的银行卡信息。本应用可能接收用于开通或恢复 Premium 所必需的购买状态或权益状态。

5. 第三方服务

Universal Downloader 仅在执行用户主动请求的功能所必需的情况下连接第三方网站或媒体服务。第三方服务独立运营，并适用其各自的服务条款和隐私规则。

6. 个人信息共享与出售

Universal Downloader 不会出售个人信息。仅在提供应用功能、处理购买、响应支持请求、遵守法律要求或防止滥用所必需的情况下，相关信息才可能由相应服务提供方处理或共享。

7. 保存期限

应用本地设置和安装标识符通常保存在设备上，直至您清除应用数据或卸载应用。您主动发送的支持或诊断信息仅在调查问题或履行法律义务所合理需要的期限内保存。应用商店保留的交易记录受相应商店政策约束。

8. 未成年人隐私

Universal Downloader 并非专门面向儿童设计的应用，开发者不会通过本应用主动要求儿童提供个人信息。

9. 安全措施

我们采取合理的技术和管理措施，以降低信息遭到未经授权访问、修改、披露或丢失的风险。但任何电子存储或传输方式均无法保证绝对安全。

10. 您的个人信息权利

根据您所在地区适用的法律，您可能有权查询、更正、删除、限制处理、提出异议，或对与本应用相关的个人信息行使其他权利。

11. 中国大陆用户说明

本应用的大部分应用设置、试用状态、推荐状态及安装标识符保存在您的设备本地。为完成您主动请求的下载，您输入的链接可能会发送至对应的第三方内容服务；付款及订阅信息由 HUAWEI AppGallery 等相应应用商店处理。本应用不会出售个人信息，也不会在未经您主动操作的情况下发送问题报告。
