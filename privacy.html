Privacy Policy — Universal Downloader

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Universal Downloader is developed and published by Zubair Abbas. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use Universal Downloader.

Information the app may process

  • An app-generated installation identifier used for trial, referral, eligibility, and anti-abuse controls.
  • Referral information such as referral code, source, status, and reward state.
  • Technical information such as app version, Android version, device model, and diagnostics used for troubleshooting.
  • Premium entitlement or purchase status when paid membership features are used. Full payment-card details are not collected by Universal Downloader.
  • URLs supplied by the user for downloading supported media.
  • Optional problem-report information voluntarily submitted by the user.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide app functions, manage trials, referrals and Premium access, prevent abuse, save app settings, diagnose problems, and meet store, security, and legal requirements.

Local storage

Downloaded files are stored on the user's device, normally in the UniversalDownloader folder inside Downloads. Local settings, trial state, referral state, and preferences may also be stored on the device.

Payments

If Premium membership is offered, payment processing is handled by the applicable app store or payment provider, including HUAWEI AppGallery where applicable. Universal Downloader does not receive or store full payment-card information. The app may receive purchase status or entitlement information required to activate and restore Premium features.

Third-party services

Universal Downloader processes links supplied by the user and may connect to third-party websites or media platforms only as needed to perform the requested function. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. Users are responsible for downloading only content they own, are authorized to download, or are otherwise permitted to save.

Sharing and sale of data

Universal Downloader does not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when necessary to operate app functionality, process purchases, respond to support requests, comply with law, or protect users and the app from abuse.

Data retention

Local app data is generally retained on the device until it is cleared, reset, or removed. Support or diagnostic information voluntarily sent by the user may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the issue or comply with legal obligations.

Children

Universal Downloader is not intended only for children and is not designed as a children's app.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
